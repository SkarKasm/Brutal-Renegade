Brutal Renegade Alpha V0.052 | 17 November 2026
Windows x86 mod overlay | Flatscreen and OpenXR

1. Make a copy of a working Command & Conquer: Renegade installation.
2. Extract the ZIP. Drag everything INSIDE Game into that copy beside
   Renegade.exe. Merge Data and confirm the supplied replacements.
3. Run Play Brutal Renegade.exe or Play Brutal Renegade VR.exe.
   Both launchers let you choose Flatscreen or VR. Start OpenXR first for VR.
   Your existing installation supplies the original maps, archives and runtime.
   To uninstall, return to your original game folder.

In VR, left stick click opens the pause panel. Right trigger clicks the menu.
Use X or right stick click for vehicles and ladders. Left stick climbs.
A ejects a magazine. Take a replacement with off-hand grip and insert it.
Hold right grip for the wheel. Aim with the right stick and release to equip.
From the first wheel page, tap left trigger for campaign Quick Actions.
Select up to save or down to load and release right grip to confirm.
Right trigger places remote C4 and left trigger detonates it.
The EVA shows health, armour, ammo, map, and the objective compass.

Use the former LAN entry for the experimental offline Command & Conquer mode.
F6 toggles tactical view. WASD pans, the scroll wheel zooms, drag selects,
and right-click orders selected units.

The build includes vanilla weapons, voxel wounds, blood soaking, blast and
fire effects, and geometry and texture upgrades. This package uses original
procedural blood sprites and decals. The private SoF2 assets are omitted.
The optional local SoF2 converter is included in the separate source archive.
RenHD is not required for this alpha. Map Studio is a separate prototype.
Online multiplayer has not been validated. For headset timing diagnostics,
set FrameTiming=1 under [VR] in renegade_vr.ini. The CSV appears beside the exe.
